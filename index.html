About us

A space where men heal, rebuild and rediscover their purpose.

In today’s society it often feels like men have been left behind. Modern men face unprecedented pressures, yet frequently walk the journey alone, with no one to stand in their corner.

Kikosi cha Wanaume was founded so that men are not judged, criticized or misunderstood, but welcomed into a dedicated, sacred space of brotherhood.

Bega kwa bega, hakuna anayeachwa nyuma. Shoulder to shoulder, no man is left behind.

Message from the founder

“We refused to sit back any longer. We chose to create a dedicated space where men come to heal, rebuild, and rediscover their purpose.”

Apostle Ambassador Kelvin Nuthu Waithaka
Founder & National Chairman

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Kikosi cha Wanaume Kenya?

Kikosi cha Wanaume Kenya, also known as the Kenya Men Association, is a brotherhood where men heal, rebuild and rediscover their purpose without being judged, criticized or misunderstood. Our motto is “Restore the Pillar.”

Who can attend the summits?

Men from all walks of life are welcome. You can bring guests, just send their names along with yours when you register.

When and where is the next gathering?

The Ignite Men Breakfast & Growth Summit is on Saturday, 14 November 2026 at KICC, Nairobi. We start at 9:00 AM sharp, so please arrive early.

How do I register?

Write your full name on the list in the group, and send the names of any guests you are bringing. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +254 785 904 087.

What will we discuss at the Ignite Summit?

Our pillars include wealth creation and financial management, mental health and emotional stability, fatherhood and legacy, mentoring the next generation, and high-impact panels with national and corporate leaders.

How can I support the summit?

Money, ideas, time and new friends are all welcome, and no amount is considered small. Our budget target is Ksh 250,000, and leaders are asked to give Ksh 2,000 each. Message us on WhatsApp to find out how to give.

How do I take up a leadership role?

Some dockets are still open, including Administrative Secretary and Security & Intelligence. Tell us your area of interest. We also run leadership training for a month, on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9:00 to 10:00 PM.
